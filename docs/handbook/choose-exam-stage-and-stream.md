# Choose the exam, stage and NICL stream

Start by selecting the target exam, then its Prelims or Mains stage. NICL also distinguishes Generalist and Information Technology streams. These choices determine the available paper blueprints and question pools; a familiar page layout does not mean every paper has the same structure.

Check the paper title, subjects, question count and timing before launching a simulation. Change the exam selection before building a practice set if you intend to prepare for a different examination. Attempts retain their exam and stream identity so the performance view can show the relevant history.

This guide describes the application's current configuration. It is not a new recruitment notification. Use the sourced exam notes and the relevant official notice when checking current eligibility, dates or rule changes; do not infer those from a previously saved mock attempt.
